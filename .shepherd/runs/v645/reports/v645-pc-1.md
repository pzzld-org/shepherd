## Classification

Architectural — it fixes the tier authority model, so it routes to `@critic` in the

## Prior state

`agents/conductor.md` ALREADY grants both, in four places:

| Site | Grant |
|---|---|
| `agents/conductor.md:160` | "**Exempt: your OWN `{run_dir}/lanes/{lane}/`** — the guard allows lane-plan custody writes there"; "**Commits AND your lane-branch push are yours**" |
| `agents/conductor.md:162` | "In-lane commits and your lane-branch push are yours (#1); `TEAMMATE-GIT-WRITE` covers cross-lane integration, not your lane commit/push" |
| `agents/conductor.md:187` | "you commit and push your OWN lane branch (#222)" |
| `agents/conductor.md:79` | "you commit and push your OWN lane branch — `TEAMMATE-GIT-WRITE` covers cross-lane integration, not your lane commit/push" |

Exactly one site contradicts them:

| Site | Text |
|---|---|
| `skills/shepherd/SKILL.md:89` | "Git custody is root-exclusive: a teammate that runs `git rebase`/`merge`/**`push`**/`worktree` halts `TEAMMATE-GIT-WRITE`" |

`SKILL.md` is the contract file every tier reads first, so its sentence wins in practice
over four correct statements buried in a role profile. Root itself obeyed the wrong one
this session: the `l7-substrate` lane brief was authored with "You may NOT commit, push,
rebase, merge, or touch worktrees", which contradicts `conductor.md` and is the defect
reproducing itself inside the same run that found it.

## Disposition

FIX-THIS-RUN. Two parts, and the second outranks the first:

1. **Prose** — `skills/shepherd/SKILL.md:89` narrows `TEAMMATE-GIT-WRITE` to cross-lane
   integration (rebase / merge / cherry-pick onto dev, `branch -d`, worktree lifecycle),
   matching `conductor.md`. Lane commit and lane-branch push are the conductor's.
   BLOCKED until `l7-substrate` lands — that lane holds `skills/shepherd/SKILL.md`.

2. **Enforcement** — per DF-68, a declaration is not a capability. Before any prose lands,
   PROVE with positive and negative controls that the runtime actually permits a lane-plan
   write and a lane-branch push, and still denies cross-lane integration and sibling
   artifact writes. If the guards disagree with the grant in either direction, the guard is
   the defect and the prose fix alone would ship a lie.
